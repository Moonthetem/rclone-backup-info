PRIVACY POLICY · PERSONAL USE

How this backup integration handles information

Effective date: 30 September 2026. This policy describes the personal VPS-backup use of the Google OAuth app named rclone.

Information used

The integration handles VPS incident backup records, including incident states, timestamps and operational receipts. Google OAuth provides the access tokens needed to create and restore backup files. These credentials are kept in restricted files on the owner's machines.

Purpose and storage

The information is used to create backups, verify recovery and retain recovery copies. Backup files are stored on the owner's VPS and in the Google Drive account chosen by the owner. Google processes the files and authentication under its privacy policy.

Sharing and sale

The backup utility does not sell backup data or use it for advertising. It transfers backup contents to Google Drive for the owner's storage and recovery. Backup folders are kept private to the owner.

Retention and control

The utility keeps recent local recovery sets and removes redundant local checkpoints only after verifying a downloaded copy. Drive copies remain under the owner's control. The owner can remove Drive files and revoke this app's access through Google account connections.

This information website

These pages are static information pages with no sign-in form or embedded analytics. The hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs under its own privacy policy.

Contact

For questions about this personal integration or a request concerning its backup files, use the user support email displayed on Google's OAuth consent screen.
